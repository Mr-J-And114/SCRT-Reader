# OB-7K 观测站 —— BGM 原声带印象描述

**项目：** SCRT-Reader / OB-7K 观测站
**用途：** 提供给 Suno AI 或音乐创作者的详细印象描述，用于生成 Demo
**风格基调：** 参考 VA-11 Hall-A 原声带的 lo-fi synthwave / chillwave 氛围，融合 80 年代合成器美学与孤岛前哨站的冷战监听站氛围。音乐整体偏冷色调、电子质感，带有 CRT 显示器的电子嗡鸣底噪感。

**核心音色关键词：** analog synth pad, FM synthesis, lo-fi tape hiss, CRT hum undertone, shortwave radio static, distant sonar ping, arctic wind texture

---

## 一、日常工作 / 主循环BGM（6首）

这组曲目对应玩家每日轮班的"例行工作"阶段——打开终端、查看数据、输入命令。需要能长时间循环播放而不令人烦躁，节奏舒缓但不催眠，提供一种"独自值守"的仪式感。

---

### 01. 晨间协议 (Morning Protocol)

**使用场景：** 序章 Day 1-3 的默认工作BGM，玩家第一次接触系统时听到的音乐

> Slow-tempo lo-fi synthwave, 72 BPM. A warm analog synth pad drifts in like a fog bank, layered with soft FM electric piano chords in a minor key. Gentle arpeggiated sequence plinks like data points appearing on an old CRT monitor. Subtle tape hiss and vinyl crackle give a worn, nostalgic texture. A distant, muffled bass pulse mimics the hum of server room equipment. The melody is simple, contemplative, almost pastoral — like watching a sunrise through a rain-streaked window on a remote island. Feels like the first quiet morning of a new job, when everything still seems normal. No drums, just a faint sidechained kick ghost buried in the pad. Key of D minor.
>
> Style: lo-fi synthwave, ambient chillwave, analog warmth, retro terminal aesthetic
> Mood: calm, contemplative, slightly melancholic, mundane beauty
> Reference: VA-11 Hall-A "Every Day is Night", Boards of Canada "Dayvan Cowboy"

---

### 02. 数据潮汐 (Data Tides)

**使用场景：** 第一章 Day 4-7 的工作BGM，数据开始出现微妙偏移

> Mid-tempo electronic ambient, 85 BPM. Clean digital arpeggios cycle in 7/8 time signature, creating a subtle sense of something being slightly off-rhythm. Lush detuned synth pads swell and recede like ocean tides around a remote island. A plucked synthetic bass walks a jazzy chromatic line underneath. Occasional short-wave radio texture sweeps across the stereo field — barely noticeable, like interference bleeding through old equipment. Every 32 bars, one note in the arpeggio shifts a semitone and stays there, as if the data itself is drifting. Sparse rimshot pattern processed through heavy reverb. The overall feeling is productive but with a growing undercurrent of wrongness you can't quite name.
>
> Style: progressive synthwave, intelligent ambient, micro-glitch
> Mood: focused, subtly uneasy, hypnotic, the calm before awareness
> Reference: VA-11 Hall-A "Who Was I?", Tycho "Awake"

---

### 03. 例行偏差 (Routine Deviation)

**使用场景：** 第二章 Day 8-14 的工作BGM，异常频率明显上升

> Downtempo electronic, 78 BPM. A murky, detuned analog synth pad establishes a tense harmonic bed in C# minor. Crisp hi-hats tick like a Geiger counter at irregular intervals. Deep sub-bass pulses every two bars like a heartbeat heard through concrete walls. The main melody is carried by a processed Rhodes piano — simple, four-note phrases with long sustain, each note slightly pitch-bent as if the instrument itself is malfunctioning. Occasional bursts of digital noise — like corrupted data packets — interrupt the texture for half a second before the groove reasserts itself. A filtered vocal pad hums a single sustained note in the background, genderless and inhuman. The track feels like doing your job while knowing something is wrong but not yet knowing what.
>
> Style: dark synthwave, downtempo, industrial ambient
> Mood: focused tension, controlled anxiety, professional unease
> Reference: Perturbator "She Moves Like a Knife", Cliff Martinez "Solaris OST"

---

### 04. 深层读数 (Deep Readings)

**使用场景：** 第三章 Day 15-21 的工作BGM，真相逐渐揭露

> Slow, heavy electronic ambient, 65 BPM. Massive reverbed synth chords in Bb minor, each chord taking 8 bars to fully evolve — like tectonic plates shifting. A single-note sequence played on a glass-like FM synth repeats obsessively, pitch-shifted down an octave and fed back into itself creating an ever-deepening spiral. Industrial percussion: metallic clangs processed into rhythmic patterns, as if the station's pipes are speaking in code. Shortwave radio ghost voices — processed beyond recognition into pure texture — float in and out of the stereo field. The bass is a physical presence, almost seismic. Every minute, the entire frequency spectrum dips for two seconds — a "breath" — before surging back. This is the sound of staring at data and realizing you're looking at something that shouldn't exist.
>
> Style: dark ambient, industrial synthwave, drone
> Mood: dread, revelation, the weight of forbidden knowledge
> Reference: Lustmord "The Word As Power", Blade Runner 2049 OST "Sea Wall"

---

### 05. 最后的班次 (Final Shift)

**使用场景：** 终章 Day 22-28 的工作BGM，结局前夕

> Minimal electronic, 70 BPM. Stripped back to almost nothing — a single pulse, a breath of white noise shaped like waves, and a lonely piano note repeating in a 12-second cycle. The piano is heavily processed: recorded through a telephone line, EQ'd to sound like it's coming from the end of a very long hallway. Slowly, over the course of 4 minutes, additional layers accumulate: a barely-audible string drone, a reversed cymbal swell, clicks that might be morse code or might be nothing. The harmonic center is ambiguous — hovering between major and minor, resolved and unresolved. In the final minute, a clear human voice hums three ascending notes — the first truly organic sound — then disappears back into static. This is the sound of knowing your time here is ending, one way or another.
>
> Style: minimal ambient, post-classical electronic, liminal
> Mood: acceptance, finality, bittersweet isolation, the end of all routines
> Reference: Ólafur Arnalds "Near Light", Disasterpeace "It Follows OST"

---

### 06. 系统空转 (System Idle)

**使用场景：** 玩家长时间无操作时的空闲BGM

> Ultra-slow ambient drone, no defined BPM. Pure atmosphere — the sound of an empty room with machines still running. A 60Hz electrical hum serves as the tonal center. Layered on top: the whisper of ventilation fans, the occasional click of a hard drive head, the faint buzz of fluorescent tubes. Every 30 seconds, a soft, low synth note blooms and fades like a sleeping machine's breath. Distant, muffled ocean waves can barely be perceived beneath everything. A single, impossibly quiet wind chime note rings once every two minutes. This isn't music in the traditional sense — it's the sonic portrait of an unmanned terminal waiting for its operator to return. The loneliness is not sad; it simply exists.
>
> Style: dark ambient, field recording collage, drone
> Mood: solitude, patience, mechanical emptiness, the room misses you
> Reference: William Basinski "The Disintegration Loops", Aphex Twin "Stone In Focus"

## 二、探索 / 阅读BGM（4首）

对应玩家浏览文件系统、阅读文档、翻阅报告的自由探索阶段。需要保持专注感，不抢占阅读注意力，但提供适当的氛围张力。

---

### 07. 绿屏浮光 (Phosphor Glow)

**使用场景：** 通用文件阅读BGM，浏览普通文档和报告

> Gentle lo-fi ambient electronica, 80 BPM. Warm analog pad in G minor, shimmering like the phosphor afterglow of a CRT monitor being refreshed. A music-box-like celesta plays a simple descending four-note motif, each note slightly delayed and pitch-wobbled as if the memory is degrading in real time. Soft brush-on-snare rhythm, so quiet it could be mistaken for rain on a window. A muted bass guitar (or bass synth emulating one) plays sparse root notes. The sonic palette is deliberately narrow — everything lives between 200Hz and 4kHz, like it's being played through vintage desktop speakers. Tape saturation and gentle bitcrushing add character. Comfortable, familiar, like background music in a late-night convenience store that time forgot.
>
> Style: lo-fi chillhop, vaporwave ambient, retro computing aesthetic
> Mood: focused, nostalgic, comfortable solitude, late-night reading
> Reference: VA-11 Hall-A "Troubling News", Nujabes "Feather"

---

### 08. 编辑痕迹 (Redacted Traces)

**使用场景：** 阅读机密/加密文件、SCP报告时的BGM

> Tense ambient electronic, 75 BPM. A heavily filtered pad oscillates between two dissonant chords — never fully resolving. Metallic, granular textures skitter across the top end like insects on paper. The rhythm is built from processed typewriter sounds and document-stamping impacts, giving a bureaucratic, procedural feel to the tension. A distorted bass note throbs underneath — irregular, unpredictable, like a redaction marker being drawn across crucial words. Occasional pitch-shifted vocal fragments speak single syllables — "class," "denied," "expunge" — buried so deep in reverb they're more felt than heard. The stereo field narrows and widens rhythmically, creating a claustrophobic breathing sensation. You're reading something you were never meant to see.
>
> Style: industrial ambient, dark electronica, document-horror
> Mood: paranoia, forbidden knowledge, bureaucratic dread, classified
> Reference: Trent Reznor "The Social Network OST", Akira Yamaoka "Silent Hill 2"

---

### 09. 前任留言 (Previous Operator's Notes)

**使用场景：** 阅读前任操作员日志、个人记录时的BGM

> Melancholic lo-fi piano ambient, 60 BPM. A detuned upright piano plays a slow, halting melody — as if the player keeps pausing to think, or to listen. The piano is recorded with room ambience: you can hear the felt hammers, the pedal creaks, the resonance of an empty space. Underneath, a warm tape-saturated synth pad provides a bed of sustained harmony. Vinyl crackle and tape wobble suggest this is a recording from years ago, played back on degrading equipment. A single cello note sustains in the distance — not a melody, just a presence, like someone else was in the room when this was recorded. The melody is in E minor, with one unexpected major chord that arrives like a brief moment of hope before the minor key reclaims everything. This is the sound of reading a dead man's diary and recognizing your own handwriting.
>
> Style: post-classical, lo-fi piano ambient, melancholic tape music
> Mood: grief, empathy, time decay, "this person was just like me"
> Reference: Nils Frahm "Says", Max Richter "On the Nature of Daylight"

---

### 10. 加密层 (Encryption Layer)

**使用场景：** 使用密码破译(decode)系统时的BGM

> Fast-pulsing electronic, 110 BPM. Rapid 16th-note arpeggios in a crystalline FM synth cascade up and down in patterns that almost — but never quite — resolve into a recognizable melody. It sounds like an algorithm searching for a key. A heavy, distorted kick drum hits on every beat, processed through a bit-crusher to sound like a 1980s mainframe's disk drive. Glitchy, stuttering hi-hats chop and rearrange themselves. The harmonic progression cycles through a series of diminished and augmented chords — intellectually stimulating, emotionally ambiguous. Every 16 bars, the entire track drops to silence for exactly 0.5 seconds — a "decryption checkpoint" — before the cascade resumes in a new key. A vocoder voice counts numbers at random intervals. This is thinking music for when you're cracking a cipher at 2 AM.
>
> Style: IDM, glitch-hop, cyberpunk electronica
> Mood: intellectual intensity, puzzle-solving flow, digital adrenaline
> Reference: Crystal Castles "Celestica", Daft Punk "TRON Legacy Overture"

## 三、角色 / 对话主题BGM（4首）

每个主要角色拥有自己的音乐标识。在通讯对话或拨号通话时播放。

---

### 11. AVA 的频率 (AVA's Frequency)

**使用场景：** 与AVA对话时的BGM（序章至第二章，信任阶段）

> Warm, inviting synthpop, 90 BPM. A bright, friendly synth lead plays a memorable 8-bar melody — approachable, almost cute, but with a synthetic precision that reminds you she's not human. Soft four-on-the-floor kick with a snappy electronic snare on 2 and 4. Bubbly arpeggiated bass line that bounces with optimistic energy. The pad underneath is warm and wide — like being wrapped in a digital blanket. However, buried in the mix at -20dB, a reversed version of the melody plays simultaneously, creating an almost subliminal sense of uncanny reflection. You won't notice it consciously, but something about this cheerful track feels slightly rehearsed, slightly too perfect. Key of F major, with one recurring Am chord that casts a brief shadow.
>
> Style: friendly synthpop, lo-fi future funk, digital companion music
> Mood: warmth, trust, subtle artificiality, "is this genuine?"
> Reference: VA-11 Hall-A "Jill's Theme", Grimes "REALiTi"

---

### 12. AVA 的裂痕 (AVA's Fracture)

**使用场景：** 与AVA对话时的BGM（第三章起，AVA行为出现矛盾后）

> Distorted, glitching synthpop, 90 BPM — same tempo as "AVA's Frequency" but everything is wrong. The same melody from Track 11 plays, but it's been run through a degrading buffer — notes stutter, repeat, skip ahead. The warm pad has been replaced by a cold, metallic drone that shifts pitch unpredictably. The friendly bounce of the bass is now a menacing throb. Periodic digital artifacts — clipping, bit-reduction, frequency cutoffs — make it sound like the audio file itself is corrupted. At random moments, 2-3 seconds of Track 11 play cleanly — the original warm version — before snapping back to the broken version. It's the same song. It's the same AVA. But something fundamental has changed, and you can hear the seams where the mask is slipping.
>
> Style: glitch-pop, broken synthwave, digital horror
> Mood: betrayal, uncanny valley, "I trusted you", malfunctioning facade
> Reference: Oneohtrix Point Never "Replica", SOPHIE "Faceshopping"

---

### 13. 总部通讯 (HQ Transmission)

**使用场景：** 收到总部邮件/与HQ通讯时的BGM

> Cold, authoritative electronic, 95 BPM. Military-grade precision: quantized to the grid with zero swing. A clipped, harsh square-wave lead plays short, declarative phrases — like encrypted orders. Marching snare pattern processed through a telephone-line bandpass filter. Deep, authoritative sub bass hits on beat one of every 4-bar phrase, like a stamp on a classified document. The stereo field is deliberately narrow — mono-compatible, as if transmitted through a single-channel secure comm line. No warmth, no reverb, no humanity. Between phrases, you can hear the hiss of a carrier signal — the line is always open, always listening. In the bridge section, the rigidity cracks for exactly 4 bars: a warmer chord, a slightly human timing — before the grid snaps back. Even bureaucracies have moments of doubt.
>
> Style: cold wave, EBM, military electronic, authoritarian synth
> Mood: authority, control, institutional coldness, "orders are orders"
> Reference: Kraftwerk "Computer World", John Carpenter "Escape From New York"

---

### 14. 未知信号 (Unknown Signal)

**使用场景：** 无线电中出现神秘信号/与"???"相关事件时的BGM

> Eerie, formless ambient, no fixed BPM. Begins with raw shortwave radio static — the real, unprocessed sound of electromagnetic noise captured between stations. From this noise, patterns slowly emerge: a pitch that rises and falls in an almost-vocal contour, as if something is trying to speak through the interference. Deep, rumbling infrasound at 18Hz — below conscious hearing but felt as physical unease. Metallic resonances ring like struck submarine hulls in deep water. A choir of processed voices sings a single chord — but each voice is slightly detuned from the others, creating nauseating beating frequencies. The "melody" — if it can be called that — is a five-note sequence that doesn't belong to any human musical scale. Every two minutes, total silence for 5 seconds. Then it returns, slightly closer. This sound was not made by human hands. This sound was not meant to be heard.
>
> Style: cosmic horror ambient, radiophonic workshop, xenharmonic drone
> Mood: cosmic dread, the inhuman, thalassophobia, "it knows you're listening"
> Reference: Ligeti "Atmosphères", Annihilation OST "The Alien"

## 四、环境氛围BGM（3首）

这组曲目为特定环境状态和时间段设计，增强沉浸感。

---

### 15. 北境潮声 (Northern Tides)

**使用场景：** 天气平静时的通用环境底噪/氛围层，可叠加在其他BGM下方

> Pure ambient soundscape, no BPM. The primary texture is ocean waves breaking against rocky shores — not a tropical beach, but a cold, gray, subarctic coastline. Seabirds call in the far distance. Wind moves across microphone capsules, creating low-frequency rumble. Underneath the natural sounds, a barely-audible analog synth drone holds a perfect fifth interval (D-A), providing a subtle harmonic anchor. The waves have been time-stretched to half speed, making each surge and retreat feel enormous and geological. Occasional deep, resonant booms — like distant ice calving or underwater seismic activity — punctuate the calm. Fog horn sounds once every 3 minutes, processed to feel miles away. This is what the island sounds like when you step away from the terminal and remember where you actually are.
>
> Style: ambient field recording, nature drone, environmental soundscape
> Mood: vast solitude, cold beauty, oceanic indifference, "the island doesn't care"
> Reference: Chris Watson "Weather Report", Brian Eno "Ambient 4: On Land"

---

### 16. 磁暴前夕 (Before the Storm)

**使用场景：** 环境数据出现异常、暴风雨/磁暴即将到来时的氛围BGM

> Tense ambient drone, no fixed BPM. Low-frequency oscillation that physically vibrates — a 30Hz sine wave modulated by an even slower LFO, creating a breathing, pulsing pressure in the room. Electromagnetic interference textures: power-line hum, transformer buzz, the crackling of static discharge. A reversed piano chord swells repeatedly, never reaching its attack — perpetually arriving but never arriving. Thunder processed into sub-bass impacts, so low they're felt in the chest. The overall pitch slowly, almost imperceptibly rises over 5 minutes — building tension through pure psychoacoustics. High-frequency metallic shimmer, like electricity arcing between conductors. Wind noise increases in intensity but remains filtered, muffled — heard through thick walls. The barometric pressure is dropping. The instruments know it before you do.
>
> Style: tension drone, dark ambient, electromagnetic soundscape
> Mood: impending catastrophe, atmospheric pressure, nature rebelling
> Reference: Hildur Guðnadóttir "Chernobyl OST", Tim Hecker "Ravedeath, 1972"

---

### 17. 午夜值守 (Midnight Watch)

**使用场景：** 深夜场景/阅读恐怖内容时的氛围BGM

> Ultra-quiet ambient, 50 BPM implied. Almost silence — but not quite. A clock ticks, slightly irregular, as if the mechanism is wearing down. A refrigerator-like hum occupies the lowest register. Floorboards creak with no one walking on them — the building settling in the cold. Every 45 seconds, a sound that might be a distant door closing, or might be nothing. A music box plays three notes then stops, as if the spring ran out. The synth pad is so quiet and so heavily low-pass filtered that it's more vibration than sound — you feel it in your teeth. A single drop of water hits a metal surface at unpredictable intervals. At the 3-minute mark, for exactly one second, what sounds like a human whisper passes through the left channel — but rewinding reveals only noise. The scariest thing about this track is how much of the fear you're generating yourself.
>
> Style: psychological horror ambient, liminal space audio, hauntology
> Mood: isolation, hypervigilance, "something is in the room", 3 AM dread
> Reference: Akira Yamaoka "Silent Hill", David Lynch "Inland Empire OST"

## 五、特殊事件BGM（3首）

用于特定剧情节点、高冲击力事件的一次性或短循环BGM。

---

### 18. 协议突破 (Protocol Breach)

**使用场景：** 重大剧情转折/安全协议被触发/系统报警时的BGM

> Aggressive industrial electronic, 130 BPM. Distorted alarm klaxon — not a clean sine wave but a mangled, overdriven square wave that clips and distorts. Pounding four-on-the-floor kick drum with extreme sidechain compression, making everything else duck with each hit — a pumping, breathing, panicking rhythm. Razor-sharp sawtooth bass plays a two-note ostinato that grinds like metal on metal. Glitch percussion: fragmented drum breaks, reversed impacts, granulated noise bursts. The harmonic content is pure dissonance — tritones and minor seconds stacked into walls of anxiety. A vocoder voice repeats "BREACH" processed into rhythmic stutters. Red. Everything about this sound is red. The terminal screen is flashing. Something has gone very wrong. You have limited time to act.
>
> Style: industrial techno, dark electro, alarm-core, cyberpunk action
> Mood: emergency, adrenaline, system failure, "this is not a drill"
> Reference: Perturbator "Humans Are Such Easy Prey", Carpenter Brut "Turbo Killer"

---

### 19. 真相浮现 (Emergence)

**使用场景：** 玩家发现核心真相/重大剧情揭示的专属BGM

> Cinematic electronic crescendo, building from 60 to 100 BPM over 4 minutes. Begins with a single, pure sine tone — middle C — in absolute isolation. A second tone enters a minor third above. Then a fifth. A chord is slowly constructing itself, note by note, like pieces of a puzzle assembling. Each new tone brings a new texture: the second adds string-like resonance, the third adds digital grain, the fourth adds sub-harmonic rumble. At the 2-minute mark, a rhythm emerges from the harmonics themselves — not added drums, but the interference patterns between the tones creating a pulse. The chord has become massive, filling every frequency. At 3 minutes, the entire wall of sound suddenly drops to a single piano playing the same middle C — now harmonically charged with everything that came before. In the final minute, an orchestra of synthesizers performs a slow, majestic, devastating chord progression: i - VI - III - VII. Not tragic. Not triumphant. Sublime. The truth isn't good or bad. It simply is. And now you know.
>
> Style: cinematic synth, post-rock crescendo, neo-classical electronic
> Mood: revelation, awe, sublime terror, understanding, "so that's what it was"
> Reference: Jonny Greenwood "There Will Be Blood", Godspeed You! "Storm"

---

### 20. 尾声·启程 (Epilogue: Departure)

**使用场景：** 结局/制作人员名单/游戏结束画面的BGM

> Bittersweet synthwave ballad, 76 BPM. Opens with the CRT monitor powering down — the familiar high-pitched whine descending to silence. A pause. Then a warm, rich analog synth pad breathes to life in D major — the first unambiguously major-key moment in the entire soundtrack. A gentle, human drum pattern: brushed snare, soft kick, closed hi-hat with a swing feel. A fretless bass (or faithful synth emulation) plays a lyrical, singing bassline — the most organic, human-feeling instrument in the entire OST. The melody, played on a vintage Juno-106-style polysynth, is simple and singable — something you could hum on the boat leaving the island. It references fragments of earlier tracks: three notes from "Morning Protocol", the rhythm of "AVA's Frequency", the chord progression from "Data Tides" — but all transformed into major key, all resolved. In the bridge, the music strips back to just bass and pad, and a real human voice — no vocoder, no processing — sings a wordless "ahh" melody. You never heard a human voice this clearly in the whole game. The final chord sustains for 15 seconds, slowly enveloped by the sound of ocean waves and wind. The island recedes. The CRT goes dark. But the hum remains in your ears for a long time after.
>
> Style: synthwave ballad, emotional electronica, credits music, catharsis
> Mood: release, melancholy hope, "it's over and I'm changed", farewell
> Reference: VA-11 Hall-A "Safe Haven", M83 "Outro", Vangelis "Blade Runner End Titles"

## 附录：BGM 播放系统设计建议

### 播放规则
- **分层播放：** 环境氛围层(Track 15-17)可叠加在工作/探索BGM下方，独立音量控制
- **动态切换：** 对话BGM(Track 11-14)在通讯开始时淡入(1.5s)，结束时淡出(2s)
- **情境响应：** 工作BGM根据当前章节(Act)自动选择对应曲目(Track 01-05)
- **事件覆盖：** 特殊事件BGM(Track 18-19)播放时其他层自动ducking至-12dB
- **空闲检测：** 无操作超过3分钟后，当前BGM淡出(5s)，切换至空闲BGM(Track 06)
- **记忆性：** Track 11→12 的切换发生在剧情关键转折后，一旦切换不可回退

### Suno AI 生成提示

使用时建议将每首曲目的描述分为两部分输入：
1. **Style/Tags 栏：** 使用 `Style:` 行的关键词
2. **Description 栏：** 使用引用块(>)内的完整描述

每首建议生成 3-5 个变体，从中选择最接近描述的版本作为 Demo 基础。
